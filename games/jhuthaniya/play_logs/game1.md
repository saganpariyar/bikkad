## Game Log

**[Turn 1] Aarav**
- Hand: 18 | Pot: 0
- Play: 3 cards face-down
- Claim: "3 Queens (Begams)"
- Reality: Q♥, Q♦, 9♣ → **BLUFF**
- Priya: Pass | Rohan: Pass → Unchallenged
- Pot: 3 | Aarav hand: 15

**[Turn 2] Priya**
- Hand: 17 | Pot: 3
- Play: 2 cards face-down
- Claim: "2 Tens"
- Reality: 10♠, 10♣ → **TRUTH**
- Rohan challenges: "JHUTH!"
- Resolution: 10♠, 10♣ revealed → Honest!
- **Penalty**: Rohan picks up pot (5 cards)
- Hands: Aarav 15, Priya 15, Rohan 22 | Pot: 0

**[Turn 3] Rohan**
- Hand: 22 | Pot: 0
- Play: 4 cards face-down
- Claim: "4 Jacks (Ghulams)"
- Reality: J♥, J♠, 4♦, 6♣ → **BLUFF**
- Aarav holds 2 Jacks → calculates impossibility
- Aarav challenges: "JHUTH!"
- Resolution: 4♦ and 6♣ revealed → Caught!
- Rohan retrieves all 4 cards | Pot: 0

**[Turns 4–27 — Mid-Game]**
- Priya plays consistently honest; small pots remain.
- Aarav alternates small bluffs; mostly unchallenged.
- Rohan absorbs two large pots (8 and 12 cards).

**[Turn 28 — Endgame] Priya**
- Standings: Priya: 2, Aarav: 3, Rohan: 16 | Pot: 6
- Play: 2 cards face-down
- Claim: "2 Kings (Badshahs)" — Final play!
- Reality: K♥, K♦ → **TRUTH**
- Aarav: Pass | Rohan: Pass → Unchallenged
- **PRIYA IS SAFE! (1st Place)**

**[Turn 29] Aarav vs Rohan**
- Standings: Aarav: 3, Rohan: 16 | Pot: 8
- Aarav plays 3 cards: "3 Aces" — Final play!
- Reality: A♠, A♣, 3♦ → **BLUFF**
- Rohan challenges: "JHUTH! You wouldn't have 3 Aces!"
- Resolution: 3♦ revealed → Caught!
- Aarav picks up 8 + 3 = 11 cards | Back in game

**[Turns 30–34] Final Battle**
- Aarav plays strategic single-card discards
- Aarav successfully unloads honest pairs
- Rohan attempts 3-card bluff on 7s → Aarav calls "JHUTH" → Caught!
- Aarav plays final card honestly: "1 Seven" → Rohan passes
- **AARAV IS SAFE! (2nd Place)**
