## Game Log

**[Turns 1–13 — Mid-Game Buildup]**
- Sara: Plays stealth single-card drops, rarely challenged.
- Arjun: Conservative mix of truth and careful bluffs.
- Vikram: Occasionally absorbs medium pots (5–7 cards).
- Ananya: Aggressive challenger, successfully catches Zain twice early.
- Zain: Adapts after early catches, shifts to honest plays.

**[Turn 14 — The Massive Pot Swing]**
- Center pot has grown to 18 cards (no one dared challenge).
- Standings: Ananya: 4, Sara: 5, Arjun: 6, Vikram: 8, Zain: 9
- **Vikram** plays 3 cards: "3 Kings"
- Reality: K♥, K♠, K♣ → **TRUTH** (Vikram playing honest under pressure)
- Ananya has **zero Kings**; bets Vikram is desperate to dump garbage into the huge pot.
- Ananya challenges: **"JHUTH!"** with supreme confidence.
- Resolution: Ananya flips first card: K♥. Flips second: K♠.
- **Vikram told the absolute truth!**
- **Penalty**: Ananya picks up ALL 21 cards (18 pot + 3 played)!
- Ananya surges from **4 → 25 cards** in a single catastrophic challenge!

**[Turns 15–20]**
- Sara: Plays final card "1 Two" → unchallenged.
- **SARA IS SAFE! (1st Place)**

**[Turn 22]**
- Arjun plays 2 cards: "2 Sixes" (final)
- Truthful, unchallenged.
- **ARJUN IS SAFE! (2nd Place)**

**[Turn 26]**
- Vikram empties hand with 2 cards: "2 Tens" → passes.
- **VIKRAM IS SAFE! (3rd Place)**

**[Final Battle] Zain vs Ananya**
- Standings: Zain: 2, Ananya: 20
- Zain plays 2 cards: "2 Nines" — Final play!
- Reality: 7♣, K♦ → **BLUFF**
- Ananya is demoralized and exhausted. Passes.
- **ZAIN IS SAFE! (4th Place)**
- Ananya left with 20+ cards.
