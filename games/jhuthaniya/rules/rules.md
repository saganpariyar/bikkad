# Jhuthaniya (Bluff / Cheat / I Doubt It) — Official Rules

## Overview
- **Players**: 2–7 (ideal: 3–5). For 6–7, use a double deck.
- **Deck**: Standard 52-card deck (no jokers).
- **Objective**: Get rid of all your cards. The last player still holding cards is the **Loser (Jhuthaniya)**.

---

## Setup & Deal
1. Dealer is chosen randomly.
2. All 52 cards are shuffled and dealt clockwise one at a time.
3. Some players may receive one more card than others (e.g., 3 players → two get 17, one gets 18). This is normal.
4. Players inspect their hands privately.

---

## Turn Actions
On your turn:
1. Place **1–4 cards face-down** into the center pot (discard pile).
2. Declare your claim aloud: **"[Count] [Rank]"** (e.g., "3 Queens", "2 Kings", "1 Ace").
3. You may play **truthfully** (actual cards of that rank) or **bluff** (any cards).

---

## Challenge Window ("Jhuth!" / "Bluff!")
- Before the next player acts, **any other player** may challenge by shouting **"Jhuth!"** or **"Check!"**
- If multiple players challenge simultaneously, the one **closest to the left** of the playing player takes priority.
- If no one challenges, all players say "Pass" and the cards remain face-down in the pot.

---

## Resolving a Challenge
**If the claim was a BLUFF** (any played card doesn't match declared rank):
- The bluffing player is caught.
- The bluffing player picks up the **entire center pot** into their hand.
- Next turn starts with the challenger (or the player to the left of the bluffer).

**If the claim was TRUTHFUL** (all played cards match declared rank):
- The challenger was wrong.
- The challenger picks up the **entire center pot** into their hand.
- The honest player retains their advantage; the turn proceeds normally.

---

## Winning & Elimination
- When a player places their last card(s), they announce they are **out**.
- They are **not officially safe** until the challenge window passes:
  - If challenged and caught bluffing → they collect the pot and **stay in the game**.
  - If unchallenged, or if challenged and proven truthful → **SAFE** (exits as a winner).
- Gameplay continues among remaining players.
- The **last remaining player** holding cards is the **LOSER (Jhuthaniya)**.

---

## Human vs Bot Setup
- **1 Human + N Bots**: Player 1 (P1) is the human; remaining seats are bots.
- **Multiple Humans**: Any seat can be assigned as human — other seats become bots.
- Supports 2 to 7 total players.

---

## Key Strategy Notes
- Track which cards have already been played to detect impossible claims.
- If you hold X cards of rank R and opponent claims Y, where X + Y > 4 (single deck) → guaranteed bluff!
- Larger pots create higher stakes; challenge carefully when the pot is big.
- Endgame: Always challenge when an opponent claims to play their final cards.
