# Mindikot / Bindikot (Bandh Hukum) — Official Rules

## Overview
- **Players**: Exactly 4, in 2 fixed partnerships.
  - **Team North-South (NS)**: P1 (South/Human) & P3 (North/Bot)
  - **Team East-West (EW)**: P2 (East/Bot) & P4 (West/Bot)
  - Partners sit directly opposite each other.
- **Deck**: Standard 52-card deck.
- **Cards Per Player**: 13 each.
- **Goal**: Capture the **four "Mindis"** (10s of all suits). Winning tricks breaks ties.

---

## Card Ranking (High to Low)
**Ace > King > Queen > Jack > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2**

## The Four Mindis
- 10♠ (Spades)
- 10♥ (Hearts)
- 10♦ (Diamonds)
- 10♣ (Clubs)

---

## Dealing & The Bandh Hukum (Closed Trump)

1. **Stage 1**: Dealer distributes **5 cards face-down** to each player.
2. **Trump Placer** (player next to dealer, typically leads first):
   - Inspects their 5 cards.
   - Selects **ONE card** and places it **face-down** in the center of the table.
   - This is the **Bandh Hukum** — its suit becomes the hidden trump. Nobody sees it yet.
3. **Stage 2**: Dealer distributes the remaining **8 cards** to each player.
4. All players now hold **13 cards** (the trump placer has 12 + the face-down card).
5. The Trump Placer **leads the first trick**.

---

## Trick-Taking Rules

### Follow-Suit Obligation
- A player **must** follow the suit of the card led if they hold any cards of that suit.

### Unveiling the Bandh Hukum ("Hukum Kholo!")
When any player is **void** (has no cards) in the led suit:
1. Player announces: **"Hukum Kholo!"** (Open the Trump!)
2. The face-down center card is **flipped face-up** — its suit is now the **permanent Trump Suit**.
3. The Trump Placer takes the revealed card back into their hand.
4. The void player who triggered the reveal **must** now:
   - **Cut** (play a trump) if they have any.
   - **Discard** any off-suit card if they have no trumps.

### Winning a Trick
- **Before trump is revealed**: Highest card of the **led suit** wins.
- **After trump is revealed**: Highest **trump** wins if any trump was played; otherwise highest card of led suit.
- The trick winner leads the next trick.

## Scoring & Win Conditions

### Primary: Mindi Count (4 Mindis total)
| Mindis Captured | Result |
|----------------|--------|
| 4 – 0          | Winning team wins (possible KOT) |
| 3 – 1          | Team with 3 wins |
| 2 – 2 (Tie)    | Broken by trick count (7+ tricks wins) |

### Types of Victories
| Type          | Condition |
|---------------|-----------|
| Regular Win   | 3+ Mindis without KOT threshold |
| KOT           | 4 Mindis + 7+ tricks |
| White-Wash    | All 4 Mindis + all 13 tricks (Super KOT) |

---

## Human vs Bot Setup
- **P1 (South)** defaults to the human player.
- **P2, P3, P4** are bots by default.
- Any seat can be configured as human; remaining seats are bots.
