PART 1: GAME RULES & SPECIFICATION (BANDH HUKUM VARIANT)
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW & PLAYERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mindikot (also known as Mindi, Mendikot, Bindikot, or Katte) is a popular South 
Asian partnership trick-taking card game. 

Key Characteristics:
  - Players: Exactly 4 players seated in two fixed partnerships (Teams of 2).
    * Team 1: Player 1 (North) & Player 3 (South)
    * Team 2: Player 2 (East) & Player 4 (West)
    * Partners sit directly opposite each other.
  - Deck: Standard 52-card deck (single deck variant) or double deck (for large 
    groups; here specified for standard 52-card single deck play).
  - Cards per player: 13 cards each.
  - Key Focus: Capturing "Mindis" (the 10s of all four suits). While winning tricks 
    matters for breaking ties and declaring a "Kot", collecting the four 10s is 
    the primary determinant of victory.


2. CARD RANKING & CARD VALUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rank hierarchy in each suit (Highest to Lowest):
  Ace > King > Queen > Jack > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2

The Mindis (Tens):
  - 10 of Spades (♠10)
  - 10 of Hearts (♥10)
  - 10 of Diamonds (♦10)
  - 10 of Clubs (♣10)

Capturing these four cards into your team's trick pile is the central goal.


3. SEATING, DEALING & THE "BANDH HUKUM" (CLOSED TRUMP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the "Bandh Hukum" (Closed/Hidden Trump) variant, the trump is NOT declared 
openly by any bidder. Instead, it is placed face-down on the table and remains 
secret until a player becomes void in a led suit.

Dealing Protocol:
  1. Dealer distributes the first batch of 5 cards face-down to each player.
  2. The player to the dealer's immediate right (or left, depending on rotation; 
     standard clockwise play: player to dealer's left leads):
     - Inspects their 5 cards.
     - Selects ONE card from their hand to serve as the Trump Suit.
     - Places this card FACE-DOWN in the center of the table without revealing 
       its suit or rank to anyone. This is the **Bandh Hukum**.
  3. The dealer then completes the deal by distributing the remaining 8 cards 
     (dealt in batches of 4 and 4, or 8 directly) to each player.
  4. All players now hold 13 cards in hand (except the trump placer, who holds 12 
     in hand plus the 1 face-down trump card on the table, which they add back 
     to their hand once trump is revealed, or maintains as their card).


4. TRICK-TAKING & UNVEILING THE BANDH HUKUM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Opening Lead: The player who placed the Bandh Hukum leads the first trick.
2. Obligation to Follow Suit:
   - A player must follow the suit of the card led if they hold at least one 
     card of that suit.
3. Unveiling the Bandh Hukum (Katte):
   - When any player is VOID (holds no cards) in the led suit:
     a) The player announces: "Hukum Kholo" (Open the Trump / Reveal Trump).
     b) The face-down card in the center is turned FACE-UP for all four players 
        to see.
     c) Its suit becomes the permanent TRUMP SUIT (Hukum) for the rest of the 
        hand.
     d) The player who placed the trump retrieves the revealed card into their 
        hand (if not played immediately) or it remains assigned to them.
     e) The void player who triggered the reveal MUST now either:
        - "Cut" (Katte): Play a card of this newly revealed trump suit.
        - Discard: If they also hold no trumps, discard any off-suit card.
4. Determining the Trick Winner:
   - Before Trump is opened: The highest card of the led suit wins.
   - After Trump is opened (or in the reveal trick itself):
     * If one or more trumps are played, the HIGHEST trump wins the trick.
     * If no trump is played, the HIGHEST card of the led suit wins.
5. Next Lead: The player who wins the trick gathers the 4 cards for their team 
   and leads the next trick.


5. SCORING, WIN CONDITIONS & DEFINITION OF "KOT"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At the end of all 13 tricks, both teams examine their captured cards.

PRIMARY WIN CONDITION: MINDI COUNT (Total = 4 Mindis)
  - 4 Mindis: The team that captured all 4 Mindis wins the round.
  - 3 Mindis vs 1 Mindi: The team with 3 Mindis wins the round.
  - 2 Mindis vs 2 Mindis (Tie in Mindis):
    * The tie is broken by the NUMBER OF TRICKS won.
    * There are 13 tricks in total. The team with 7 or more tricks wins.

TYPES OF VICTORIES:
  1. Regular Win:
     - Winning 3 or 4 Mindis without meeting the threshold for a Kot.
  2. KOT (Clean Sweep / 7+ Tricks & 4 Mindis):
     - If the non-dealing team wins 4 Mindis and at least 7 tricks, it is a KOT.
     - If one team captures all 4 Mindis AND wins all 13 tricks, it is a 
       "WHITE-WASH" (52-Patti Kot / Super Kot), the highest possible victory.
  3. Defending Dealer:
     - If the dealing team loses, the deal shifts clockwise.
     - If the dealing team wins, they have defended their deal; in many house 
       rules, the same dealer deals again, or standard rotation proceeds with 
       points awarded.

PART 2: THREE STEP-BY-STEP GAME SIMULATIONS

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAME 1: TRUMP UNVEILED EARLY, DRAMATIC MINDI THEFT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Teams:
  - Team North-South (N-S) [N: Player 1, S: Player 3]
  - Team East-West   (E-W) [E: Player 2, W: Player 4]
Dealer: West (Player 4).
Trump Placer: North (Player 1, seated left of dealer).

Stage 1 Deal (5 cards each):
  - North inspects first 5 cards: [♠A, ♠K, ♠10, ♥8, ♦Q].
  - North selects ♠K as the BANDH HUKUM, placing it face-down in the center.
Stage 2 Deal:
  - Remaining cards dealt. All players have 13 cards.

Trick Progression:
  - Trick 1: North leads ♥A. East plays ♥7. South plays ♥9. West plays ♥10.
    * West is forced to play their ♥10 (Mindi!).
    * North's ♥A takes the trick.
    * N-S captures 1st Mindi (♥10). [N-S: 1 trick, 1 Mindi | E-W: 0]

  - Trick 2: North leads ♦A. East plays ♦8. South plays ♦J. West plays ♦K.
    * North wins with ♦A. [N-S: 2 tricks, 1 Mindi | E-W: 0]

  - Trick 3: North leads ♦7. East plays ♦9. South plays ♦10 (Mindi!).
    * West is VOID in Diamonds!
    * West calls: "Hukum Kholo!" (Open Trump).
    * North's face-down card is flipped: SPADES (♠) is Trump!
    * North takes ♠K back to hand.
    * West, being void in Diamonds, CUTS with ♠8!
    * West's ♠8 trumps South's ♦10.
    * E-W captures 2nd Mindi (♦10)! [N-S: 2 tricks, 1 Mindi | E-W: 1 trick, 1 Mindi]

  - Trick 4: West leads ♣K. North plays ♣8. East plays ♣10 (Mindi!). South plays ♣A.
    * South's ♣A captures East's ♣10!
    * N-S captures 3rd Mindi (♣10). [N-S: 3 tricks, 2 Mindis | E-W: 1 trick, 1 Mindi]

  - Tricks 5 - 12:
    * Intensive cross-trumping in Spades.
    * In Trick 9, North leads ♠A, drawing out East's ♠J and West's ♠10 (Mindi!).
    * N-S captures 4th Mindi (♠10)!

  - Trick 13: South wins final trick with ♥K.

Game 1 Final Results:
  - N-S Won: 8 Tricks, 3 Mindis (♥10, ♣10, ♠10)
  - E-W Won: 5 Tricks, 1 Mindi (♦10)
  - Verdict: Team North-South WINS by Mindi majority (3-1).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAME 2: 2-2 MINDI SPLIT (TIE-BREAKER DECIDED BY TRICKS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Teams:
  - Team North-South (P1 & P3)
  - Team East-West   (P2 & P4)
Dealer: North (Player 1).
Trump Placer: East (Player 2).

Deal & Bandh Hukum:
  - East inspects first 5 cards: [♥A, ♥K, ♥10, ♦7, ♣9].
  - East places ♥K face-down as the BANDH HUKUM.
  - Full hands dealt.

Trick Progression:
  - Trick 1: East leads ♣A. South plays ♣7. West plays ♣8. North plays ♣10 (Mindi!).
    * East captures North's ♣10.
    * E-W secures 1st Mindi (♣10). [E-W: 1 trick, 1 Mindi]

  - Trick 2: East leads ♦A. South plays ♦8. West plays ♦10 (Mindi!). North plays ♦K.
    * East's ♦A collects West's ♦10 safely into partnership pile.
    * E-W secures 2nd Mindi (♦10). [E-W: 2 tricks, 2 Mindis]

  - Trick 3: East leads ♦J. South plays ♦Q. West plays ♦9. North is VOID in Diamonds!
    * North calls: "Hukum Kholo!"
    * Card revealed: HEARTS (♥) is Trump.
    * North cuts with ♥8!
    * North takes the trick. [N-S: 1 trick, 0 Mindis | E-W: 2 tricks, 2 Mindis]

  - Trick 4: North leads ♠A. East plays ♠8. South plays ♠10 (Mindi!). West plays ♠9.
    * North takes trick, protecting partner's ♠10.
    * N-S secures 3rd Mindi (♠10). [N-S: 2 tricks, 1 Mindi | E-W: 2 tricks, 2 Mindis]

  - Trick 5: North leads ♠K. East plays ♠J. South plays ♠7. West plays ♠Q.
    * North wins. [N-S: 3 tricks, 1 Mindi]

  - Trick 6: North leads ♠6. East is void, cuts with ♥7. South cuts higher with ♥Q! West plays ♥9.
    * South's over-trump takes trick. [N-S: 4 tricks, 1 Mindi]

  - Trick 7: South leads ♥10 (The 4th Mindi!). West plays ♥J. North plays ♥A. East plays ♥K.
    * North wins with ♥A, pulling the last Mindi for N-S!
    * N-S secures 4th Mindi (♥10). [N-S: 5 tricks, 2 Mindis | E-W: 2 tricks, 2 Mindis]

  - Tricks 8 - 13:
    * Both teams sit deadlocked at 2 Mindis each!
    * The battle pivots completely to trick counting (target: 7 tricks).
    * South controls the remaining Clubs and takes 2 more tricks.
    * East takes 2 tricks with top trumps.
    * Final trick count: N-S: 7 tricks | E-W: 6 tricks.

Game 2 Final Results:
  - Mindis: Tied 2 - 2 (N-S has ♠10, ♥10 | E-W has ♣10, ♦10)
  - Trick Count: N-S won 7 tricks | E-W won 6 tricks
  - Verdict: Team North-South WINS via the 7-trick tie-breaker!


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAME 3: A DEVASTATING "KOT" (4 MINDIS + 10 TRICKS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Teams:
  - Team North-South (P1 & P3)
  - Team East-West   (P2 & P4)
Dealer: South (Player 3).
Trump Placer: West (Player 4).

Deal & Bandh Hukum:
  - West places ♦A face-down as BANDH HUKUM.
  - Hands completed (13 cards each).

Trick Progression:
  - Trick 1: West leads ♠K. North plays ♠7. East plays ♠A. South plays ♠10 (Mindi!).
    * East takes South's ♠10!
    * E-W captures 1st Mindi.

  - Trick 2: East leads ♣K. South plays ♣7. West plays ♣A. North plays ♣10 (Mindi!).
    * West captures North's ♣10!
    * E-W captures 2nd Mindi.

  - Trick 3: West leads ♥A. North plays ♥7. East plays ♥10 (Mindi!). South plays ♥8.
    * West collects East's protected ♥10.
    * E-W captures 3rd Mindi.

  - Trick 4: West leads ♥K. North plays ♥9. East plays ♥J. South is VOID in Hearts!
    * South declares: "Hukum Kholo!"
    * Card revealed: DIAMONDS (♦) is Trump!
    * South has no Diamonds either! Discards ♠8.
    * West's ♥K wins the trick.

  - Trick 5: West leads ♦K. North plays ♦8. East plays ♦10 (4th Mindi!). South plays ♦9.
    * E-W collects the 4th and final Mindi (♦10)!
    * All 4 Mindis secured by E-W in just 5 tricks.

  - Tricks 6 - 13:
    * East-West relentlessly play high trumps and master cards, winning 10 tricks 
      total before conceding 3 minor tricks to North-South.

Game 3 Final Results:
  - N-S: 3 Tricks, 0 Mindis
  - E-W: 10 Tricks, 4 Mindis (ALL FOUR)
  - Verdict: Team East-West inflicts a definitive KOT on Team North-South!
