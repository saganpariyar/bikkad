# Tikdi (3-Player / Teen Do Paanch / 3-2-5 / 5-3-2)

- **Status**: 🟢 **Live Now**
- **Type**: 3-Player Trick-Taking & Quota Game
- **Players**: Exactly 3 players
- **Deck**: 30 cards (A, K, Q, J, 10, 9, 8 in all 4 suits + 7♥ and 7♠)

---

## Quotas & Roles

- **Player 2 (Trump Chooser)**: 5 tricks
- **Player 3 (Non-Dealer / Bystander)**: 3 tricks
- **Player 1 (Dealer)**: 2 tricks
- **Total Tricks**: 10 tricks per round

---

## Dealing Sequence (5-3-2 Batching)

1. **Stage 1**: Dealer distributes 5 cards each. The Trump Chooser evaluates their cards and declares the Trump Suit (*Hukum*).
2. **Stage 2**: Dealer distributes 3 cards each.
3. **Stage 3**: Dealer distributes 2 cards each. (All players have 10 cards).

---

## Penalty Settlement (Blind Card Pulling)

- Any surplus/deficit carries over directly to the next round:
  `Net Score = Tricks Won - Quota`
- Creditor(s) blindly pull 1 card from Debtor(s) for each trick owed, and return 1 unwanted card back.
