================================================================================
          TIKDI (3-PLAYER CARD GAME / 3-2-5 / 5-3-2)
                   OFFICIAL RULEBOOK
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tikdi (widely known in South Asia as Teen Do Paanch / 3-2-5 / 5-3-2) is a strategic, 
trick-taking card game played by exactly 3 players using a specialized 30-card 
stripped deck.

In every deal, each player is assigned a specific quota of tricks to capture based 
on their role:
  - Trump Chooser: 5 tricks
  - Non-Dealer (Bystander): 3 tricks
  - Dealer: 2 tricks
  - Total tricks available in each hand: 10 tricks

The objective of each hand is to win at least your designated quota of tricks. 
Failing to meet your quota creates a deficit (debt) owed to players who took a 
surplus (tricks beyond their quota). This debt is collected at the start of the 
very next deal. The overall match is played either to a set surplus score (e.g., 
first to reach +5 or +10 net points) or over an agreed number of full rotations.


2. DECK COMPOSITION (30 CARDS TOTAL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The game is played using a 30-card custom deck stripped from a standard 52-card 
deck:

  - Red Hearts (Laal / H):        7, 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace  (8 cards)
  - Black Spades (Kaali / S):     7, 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace  (8 cards)
  - Black Clubs (Chidi / C):         8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace  (7 cards)
  - Red Diamonds (Cergat/It / D):    8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace  (7 cards)

Card Rank Hierarchy (Highest to Lowest):
  - In Hearts and Spades:
      Ace > King > Queen > Jack > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7
  - In Clubs and Diamonds:
      Ace > King > Queen > Jack > 10 > 9 > 8
  (Note: 2 through 6 of all suits, plus the 7 of Clubs and 7 of Diamonds, are 
   permanently removed from the deck).


3. SEATING, ROLES & ROTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Three players are seated at the table:
  - Player 1 (Dealer): Responsible for shuffling and distributing the cards.
  - Player 2 (Trump Chooser): Sits to the left (clockwise) of the dealer.
  - Player 3 (Bystander / Non-Dealer): Sits between Trump Chooser and Dealer.

Quotas:
  - Trump Chooser: 5 tricks
  - Non-Dealer:    3 tricks
  - Dealer:        2 tricks

Role Rotation:
  - After each deal completes, the role of Dealer shifts one position clockwise.


4. DEALING PROTOCOL (THE 5 - 3 - 2 SEQUENCE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The dealer thoroughly shuffles the 30-card deck and distributes the cards in 
three consecutive batches:

  Stage 1 (First 5 Cards):
    - The dealer deals 5 cards face-down to each player (15 cards total).
    - Play halts.
    - The Trump Chooser evaluates their 5 cards and declares the Trump Suit 
      (Hukum) aloud to all players.

  Stage 2 (Next 3 Cards):
    - After the trump is announced, the dealer deals 3 cards face-down to each 
      player (9 cards total).

  Stage 3 (Final 2 Cards):
    - The dealer deals 2 cards face-down to each player (6 cards total).
    - All players now hold exactly 10 cards in their hands.


5. PRE-ROUND PENALTY RESOLUTION (BLIND CARD PULLING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If debts were incurred in the previous hand, the Creditor (player with surplus) 
pulls cards blindly from the Debtor (player with deficit) before Trick 1 begins:
  - The Debtor fans out their 10 cards face-down.
  - The Creditor blindly pulls 1 card for each trick owed.
  - The Creditor inspects the pulled card(s) and returns an equal number of 
    unwanted cards from their hand back to the Debtor.


6. TRICK-TAKING RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Opening Lead: The Trump Chooser always leads the first card of Trick 1.
  2. Obligation to Follow Suit:
     - Players must follow the suit of the led card if they hold any card of that 
       suit.
  3. Trumping and Discarding:
     - If a player holds NO cards of the led suit (they are void), they may:
       a) Play any Trump card to cut (trump) the trick.
       b) Play any off-suit non-trump card (discard / pass).
  4. Determining the Trick Winner:
     - If one or more Trump cards are played, the HIGHEST rank Trump card wins.
     - If no Trump cards are played, the HIGHEST rank card of the led suit wins.
     - Off-suit non-trump cards have zero winning power.
  5. Subsequent Leads:
     - The winner of a trick gathers the 3 cards into their won-tricks pile, and 
       leads any card from their hand for the next trick.
  6. Play continues until all 10 tricks are played out.


7. SCORING & DEFICIT SETTLEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At the conclusion of the 10 tricks, each player calculates their net result:
  Net Score = (Tricks Won) - (Target Quota)

  - Net Score > 0: Player has a SURPLUS (Creditor).
  - Net Score < 0: Player has a DEFICIT (Debtor).
  - Net Score = 0: Player broke even.

Total surplus across the table always equals total deficit. Debts carry over to 
the very next round for penalty card pulling.
