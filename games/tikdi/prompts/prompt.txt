===============================================================================
               TIKDI (3-PLAYER / TEEN DO PAANCH / 3-2-5)
                     AI & ENGINE PROMPT SPECIFICATION
===============================================================================

1. GAME DOMAIN SPECIFICATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Players: Exactly 3 players (1 Human user vs 2 Rule-based/Heuristic AI agents,
   or multiplayer 2 humans + 1 bot / 3 humans).
2. Deck Composition: Exactly 30 cards:
   - Hearts (♥): 7, 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace (8 cards)
   - Spades (♠): 7, 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace (8 cards)
   - Clubs (♣): 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace (7 cards)
   - Diamonds (♦): 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace (7 cards)
   - Rank Order: A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 (7s only exist in ♥ and ♠).
3. Role Quotas (10 tricks total):
   - Trump Chooser: 5 tricks
   - Non-Dealer / Bystander: 3 tricks
   - Dealer: 2 tricks
4. Dealing Sequence (5-3-2 Batching):
   - Stage 1: Deal 5 cards each. Play pauses.
   - Trump Selection: The Trump Chooser inspects their 5 cards and declares Trump.
   - Stage 2: Deal 3 cards each.
   - Stage 3: Deal 2 cards each (all players now have 10 cards).
5. Pre-Round Penalty Resolution (Deficit Mechanism 1 - Blind Card Pulling):
   - If debts were incurred in the previous hand, the Creditor pulls N cards 
     randomly from the Debtor's 10-card hand before Trick 1 is led.
   - The Creditor inspects the pulled card(s) and returns N unwanted card(s) 
     from their hand back to the Debtor.
6. Trick-Taking Logic:
   - Trump Chooser leads Trick 1.
   - Mandatory Follow Suit: A player must follow the led suit if they have it.
   - Void Play: If void, player can cut with Trump or discard any non-trump card.
   - Trick Evaluation: Highest trump played wins; if no trump played, highest 
     card of the led suit wins.
   - Winner collects the trick and leads the next trick.
7. Post-Round Scoring & Rotation:
   - Net = (Tricks Won) - (Target Quota).
   - Record surplus/deficit debts into the persistent match ledger.
   - Rotate dealer role clockwise.

2. AI STRATEGY HEURISTICS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Trump Chooser AI: Analyzes the initial 5 cards, counts suit lengths and high 
  card points (A=4, K=3, Q=2, J=1), and chooses the strongest suit.
- Penalty Return AI: Returns the lowest non-trump card when repaying a debtor.
- Trick Play AI:
  - If leading: Prefers cash aces, high trumps, or establishes long suits.
  - If following: Plays lowest winning card if partner doesn't exist; saves high cards.
  - If void: Cuts with lowest trump if trick is high-value; otherwise discards lowest trash card.
