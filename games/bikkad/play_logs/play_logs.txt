RULES SUMMARY:
1. Standard Trick Collection (Double Sir): A team must win 2 consecutive tricks to collect the pot.
   Must follow lead suit. Off-suit without trump = 0 value. Last trick winner sweeps remaining pot.
2. Modes & Targets:
   - Regular     : Lead Team needs >=9 tricks (+9 to dealer). Dealer Team needs >=5 tricks (-18 to dealer).
   - Tera        : Declarer + Partner must win all 13 tricks. 
                   Success = 13 pts in declarer's favor. Fail (opponents win >=1 trick) = 26 pts to opponents.
                   Trump is chosen in runtime by declarer.
   - Double Tera : Declarer plays completely solo (partner sits out). Must win all 13 tricks.
                   Success = 26 pts in declarer's favor. Fail (opponents win >=1 trick) = 52 pts to opponents.
                   Trump is chosen in runtime by declarer.
3. Tug-of-War Ladder & Dealer Rotation:
   - Reach <= 0  : Dealer is FREE. Deal passes to OPPONENT at 9 points.
   - Cross >= 52 : Dealer DEFEATED (Coat). Deal passes to PARTNER at remainder (Score - 52).
