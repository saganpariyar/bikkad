  Team B: Player 2 (P2) & Player 4 (P4)

RULES SPECIFICATION:
  1. Regular Play:
     - 4 players (2 vs 2), dealer deals 5 cards first; Eldest Hand sets 1 Hidden Trump (Bandh Hukum), then remaining 8.
     - Must follow lead suit. Off-suit discard = 0 trick value.
     - Pot Collection (Double Sir): A team must win 2 CONSECUTIVE tricks to scoop the center pot. 
       Winner of Trick 13 sweeps any remaining cards left in the pot.
     - Target: Challenger/Lead team needs >= 9 tricks (+9 to dealer). Dealer team needs >= 5 tricks (-18 to dealer).
  2. Special Contract Modes:
     - Tera (13-Trick Team Sweep): Declarer + Partner must win all 13 tricks. Trump selected at runtime.
       Success = 13 pts swing | Failure (opponents take >= 1 trick) = 26 pts penalty to opponents.
     - Double Tera (13-Trick Solo): Declarer plays SOLO (Partner sits out). Must win all 13 tricks. Trump at runtime.
       Success = 26 pts swing | Failure (opponents take >= 1 trick) = 52 pts penalty to opponents (Instant Coat/Bust).
  3. Ladder & Dealer Rotation (Tug-of-War):
     - Score reaches <= 0: Dealer is FREE. Shuffle passes to next OPPONENT (P1 -> P2 -> P3 -> P4) starting at 9 pts.
     - Score crosses >= 52: Dealer is DEFEATED (Bust/Coat). Shuffle passes to PARTNER starting at remainder (Score - 52).

NOTATION:
  - Card Suits : S = Spades, H = Hearts, D = Diamonds, C = Clubs.
  - Card Ranks : A, K, Q, J, 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2.
  - *          : Winning card of the led suit (or highest trump).
  - []         : Off-suit discard (Void in led suit; 0 trick value).
  - #          : Trump card played after opening the trump suit (Kaat).
