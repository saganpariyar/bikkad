# TASK: Full-Stack Production Implementation of Bikkad (Bandh Rang Double Sir) Card Game

Build a complete, production-grade, interactive multiplayer/AI card game of **Bikkad** (also known as Bandh Rang Double Sir / Tug-of-War Court Piece). 
The game must support flexible seat configurations (1 Human + 3 AI, 2 Humans + 2 AI, 4 AI Auto-Simulation, or 4-Player Local/Network Multiplayer), integrate local **Ollama AI** agents with low-latency structured output and deterministic fallbacks, and feature a modern real-time Web UI.

---

## 1. GAME SPECIFICATION & STATE MACHINE (`rule.txt` Logic)

### A. Team Setup & Deck
- **Deck:** Standard 52-card pack without Jokers.
- **Card Hierarchy (High to Low):** A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2.
- **Seating (Clockwise):** South (P1 - Team A), West (P2 - Team B), North (P3 - Team A), East (P4 - Team B).

### B. Deal & Hidden Trump (*Bandh Hukum*)
1. Dealer deals **5 cards** clockwise to each player.
2. The Eldest Hand (player directly left of the dealer, who leads Trick 1) selects **1 card to place face-down as the Hidden Trump**.
3. Dealer distributes the remaining 8 cards to each player (13 cards total in hand).

### C. Trick Play & Void Logic
1. **Must Follow Suit:** Players must play a card of the led suit if they hold one. Off-suit discards without trump have 0 trick value.
2. **Void Options (No cards of led suit):**
   - **Discard (Sluff):** Play any off-suit card without revealing the trump (0 value).
   - **Demand the Cut (*Rang Kholna*):** Turn the hidden trump face-up for all players.
     - The demanding player **must cut with a card of the newly revealed trump suit**.
     - *Edge Case Handling:* If the demanding player is void in the led suit AND also holds zero cards of the revealed trump suit, the trump remains face-up for the table, and the player plays a standard 0-value discard.
     - Once opened, the trump suit remains active for all subsequent tricks.

### D. Double Sir Pot Accumulator
1. Individual tricks are not collected immediately; cards accumulate face-down in the **Center Pot**.
2. A team collects the entire pot only upon winning **2 consecutive tricks** (by either partner).
3. If Team B wins Trick 1, but Team A wins Trick 2, Team B's streak resets to 0, Team A gains 1 pending win, and all 8 cards remain in the pot.
4. **13th Trick Sweep:** The winner of Trick 13 automatically sweeps all remaining cards in the center pot.

### E. Game Contract Modes
1. **Regular Mode:**
   - Lead Team Target: $\ge 9$ tricks ($36+$ cards) $\rightarrow$ Adds **$+9$ points** to dealer debt.
   - Dealer Team Target: $\ge 5$ tricks ($20+$ cards) $\rightarrow$ Reduces dealer debt by **$-18$ points**.
2. **Tera Mode (13-Trick Team Challenge):**
   - Declared before Trick 1. Standard 4 players (2 vs 2). No hidden card; declarer chooses trump at runtime upon first void.
   - Declarer + Partner must win **all 13 tricks**. If opponents take $\ge 1$ trick, Tera fails immediately.
   - Early Termination: Abort the round instantly when an opponent takes a trick.
   - Scoring: Success = **$13$ points** swing in declarer's favor; Failure = **$26$ points** penalty to opponents.
3. **Double Tera Mode (13-Trick Solo Challenge):**
   - Declared before Trick 1. Declarer plays **completely SOLO** (Partner sits out; 3 active players).
   - Solo player must win **all 13 tricks**. Abort instantly if opponents take $\ge 1$ trick.
   - Scoring: Success = **$26$ points** swing in declarer's favor; Failure = **$52$ points** penalty to opponents (triggers an immediate 52-point bust).

### F. Tug-of-War Score Ladder & Dealer Rotation
Only **one active burden score** is tracked against the current dealer:
1. **Getting Free ($\le 0$ Points):** Dealer clears debt. Shuffle passes clockwise to the **opponent**, starting at **9 points**.
2. **Cap-Out Rollover ($\ge 52$ Points):** If dealer crosses 52 (e.g., $45 + 9 = 54$, or takes a $+52$ Double Tera penalty), they take a *Coat*.
   - Shuffle bypasses opponents and passes directly to that dealer's **partner**.
   - Partner inherits the deal at the exact remainder: $\text{Remainder} = \text{Current Score} - 52$ (e.g., $54 - 52 = \mathbf{2\text{ points}}$).
