### 2. Core Game Engine Implementation (`engine/`)

Implement exact state transitions based on `rule.txt`:
1. **Deck & Deal**:
   - 52 standard cards. Deal 5 cards first. Eldest hand sets 1 face-down hidden trump (`Bandh Hukum`).
   - Deal remaining 8 cards per player (13 total).
2. **Trick Resolution**:
   - Must follow suit led. Discards from other suits have 0 value.
   - If void: Player can either discard or trigger "Open Trump" (`Rang Kholna`).
   - If Trump opened: Trump suit beats all non-trump suits.
3. **Double Sir Pot Collection**:
   - Center pot accumulates cards.
   - A team must win **2 consecutive tricks** to collect the pot.
   - Streak resets if the opposing team wins a trick.
   - Winner of Trick 13 sweeps all remaining pot cards.
4. **Scoring Ladder (Tug-of-War)**:
   - Only 1 active score on the board tied to the dealer.
   - Regular Mode: Lead team wins (>=9 trk) -> `+9`. Dealer team wins (>=5 trk) -> `-18`.
   - Tera Mode (13-0 Team): Win = `-13` / `+13`; Loss (opponent takes >=1) = `+26` to opponents. Trump chosen at runtime.
   - Double Tera Mode (13-0 Solo, 1 vs 2): Win = `-26` / `+26`; Loss = `+52` penalty to opponents.
5. **Dealer Rotation**:
   - Score `<= 0` (Free) -> Shuffle passes to next OPPONENT starting at 9 points.
   - Score `>= 52` (Cap Out) -> Shuffle passes to PARTNER starting at remainder `(Score - 52)` points.

2. **Deterministic Fallback Engine**:
   - If Ollama is offline, unreachable, or returns an invalid/illegal card, fall back gracefully to a heuristic rule-based AI (`heuristic_player.py`) without crashing or halting the game.
